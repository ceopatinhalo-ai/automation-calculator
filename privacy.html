Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

At AutoFlow Tech ("we", "us", or "our"), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, storage, and processing of personal data when you interact with our website (ceopatinhalo-ai.github.io/automation-calculator) and our automated developer integrations (including TikTok for Developers Login Kit and Content Posting API).

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize our application via TikTok OAuth 2.0 or related social APIs, we may receive limited data strictly necessary for providing video scheduling services:

  • Basic Profile Information: Open ID, display name, and avatar URL (under the user.info.basic scope) to verify creator identity.
  • Content Publishing Scopes: Permissions to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish) on your behalf as explicitly initiated by you.
  • Technical Tokens: Access tokens and refresh tokens stored securely in local encrypted environment variables to maintain session continuity.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data solely for the following operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your creator profile with TikTok.
  • To schedule and publish your video content according to your configured calendar.
  • To monitor API quotas and delivery status.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or content to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Security

All authentication credentials and tokens are stored in private, air-gapped local environment vaults. We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Data Deletion & Revocation

You may revoke AutoFlow Tech's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok mobile app settings (under Security and Permissions > Apps and Services). Upon request or revocation, all associated access tokens are permanently deleted from our local storage.
